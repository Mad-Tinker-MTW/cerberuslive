# Cerberus — Profile & Dossier Notes

Captured 2026-06-29. Two parts: recommended field values for the frankydlp / f-de-la-paz dossier,
and product ideas for the dossier itself (lean into the "CIA dossier" feel). Content notes, not yet
applied.

## frankydlp / f-de-la-paz profile field recommendations

- **Performance Type** options: Open Format DJ; Live DJ Set; Original Music Showcase; MC / Host;
  Hybrid DJ + Producer Performance
- **Travel Range** (starting out): `Local • Regional (OK • TX • KS • AR)`. Later: `Nationwide • International`
- **Status**: `Available for Booking`
- **Response Time**: `Usually within 24 hours`
- **Booking Range**: avoid a dollar amount if not charging yet. Use `Negotiable` or `Contact for Quote`
- **Booking Email**: `booking@cerberuslive.studio` (until a personal management email is chosen)
- **Best For**: Clubs, Festivals, Community Events, College Events, Corporate Events, Private Parties, Live Streams
- **Crowd Fit**: `Clubs • Festivals • Corporate • Community Events • Private Events`
- **Clean Set**: `Yes • Radio Friendly Available` (tells promoters you can adjust)
- **Languages**: English; Spanish (Conversational)
- **Energy**: `Dynamic` or `High Energy` (not just "High")
- **Equipment**: Professional DJ Controller; Laptop; Microphone; Audio Interface; Performs with House PA
- **Stage Presence**: remove the stars; use `Confident, Interactive, Crowd Engaging` (stars read as self-rating)
- **Featured Track**: `Coming Soon` until there are releases. Length: `--`
- **Website**: once launched, point to `cerberuslive.studio/artists/fdelapaz` instead of a personal site

## Cerberus dossier feature ideas (product)

Lean into the dossier theme. After the Bio, add three quick-scan sections so a booker understands an
artist in about 10 seconds:

- **Artist Traits** (star ratings): Crowd Reading, Freestyle, Mixing, Songwriting, Production,
  Stage Presence, Collaboration
- **Signature Sounds** (checklist): Conscious Hip-Hop, House, Freestyle, Latin Rhythm, Funk,
  80's Throwbacks, Modern Club, Mashups, AI-Assisted Production
- **Influences**: Hip-Hop, House, Freestyle, Latin, Electronic, Rock, Synthwave, Old School, Modern Club

## "Artist DNA" radar chart (standout idea)

A circular radar / stat chart (game character-sheet style) showing attributes such as Stage Energy,
Technical Skill, Crowd Interaction, Originality, Versatility, and Improvisation. Fits the dossier
theme perfectly and gives visitors an instant visual read on a performer. A differentiator vs other
artist platforms. Candidate to build on the dossier page (data from the editor; render as SVG).
