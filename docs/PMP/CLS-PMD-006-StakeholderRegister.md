### STK-003: Underground Artists, DJs, and Performers
**Role:** Primary User and Supply Side
**Organization:** External
**Influence:** Medium
**Interest:** High
**Engagement:** Collaborate

**Description:** The artists the platform is built for. Independent acts too serious for a single shared link and too independent for a label. They supply the profiles and media that give the directory value. The self-hosted media model and Cerberus Managed agency service are aimed squarely at them.

**Communication:** Phase 0 waitlist captures interest by role. Future: managed-artist onboarding, segmented email campaigns, and the public profile experience.

---

### STK-004: Venues
**Role:** Demand Side
**Organization:** External
**Influence:** Medium
**Interest:** Medium
**Engagement:** Collaborate

**Description:** Clubs, bars, and event spaces looking for talent. They list their space, capacity, dates, and genre preferences for free, and they close the loop by booking artists through the platform. The venue-side commission (12.5%) and the booking layer (Phase 4) are built around them.

**Communication:** Phase 0 waitlist captures venue signups. Future: venue discovery dashboard, booking request flow, and direct outreach by the managed-agent service.

---

### STK-005: Fans and Listeners
**Role:** Audience
**Organization:** External
**Influence:** Low
**Interest:** Medium
**Engagement:** Inform

**Description:** Free accounts that follow artists, stream media, and buy showcase tickets. They are the draw that makes profiles and bookings worth something, and the ticket buyers whose numbers underwrite the showcase model.

**Communication:** Free signup, follow system, and discovery feed. Future: email campaigns and social sharing on artist pages.

---

### STK-006: Acquirer
**Role:** Exit Buyer
**Organization:** External
**Influence:** Low (currently)
**Interest:** Medium (assumed)
**Engagement:** Inform

**Description:** A future buyer of the platform. Acquisition is the planned exit. A buyer gets the codebase, the active artist profiles (target 500 or more), recurring MRR, the booking pipeline, and the brand and domain. Target range $50k to $500k depending on traction, acquisition-ready after Phase 4.

**Communication:** No current engagement. Future: due-diligence package assembled from the codebase, registry, metrics, and this documentation suite.
